## Services

### Web Design
**What it is:** Custom website design and development for B2B service companies.
**What the client gets:** A conversion-focused website built to generate inquiries — not just look good. Every page is architected around the buyer's path from first visit to contact form.
**Who needs this:** B2B service companies, contractors, and professional services firms where a single new client is worth $10K or more and the website is losing deals it should be winning.
**Starting price / range:** Custom-quoted based on scope.

### SEO
**What it is:** Technical SEO, keyword strategy, on-page optimization, and local search for service businesses.
**What the client gets:** Rankings for the search terms buyers use when they have budget and no vendor yet — not just the business name. Includes a $197 diagnostic entry point: find 5 revenue-impacting issues or it's free.
**Who needs this:** Local and regional service businesses that rely on inbound leads and aren't ranking for their core service terms.
**Starting price / range:** $197 SEO Diagnostic (entry point). Ongoing retainers custom-quoted.

### Lead Generation
**What it is:** Vetted B2B contact lists and outreach execution for companies that can't scale on referrals alone.
**What the client gets:** Verified decision-maker contacts matched to their ICP, delivered monthly. Ranges from raw data delivery to fully managed cold email outreach.
**Who needs this:** B2B service companies with average deal values above $25K who need a predictable pipeline beyond word-of-mouth.
**Starting price / range:** $1,995/month (Lead Data tier) to $9,000/month (Full Service).

## About / Origin Story

Social Linus was founded by Shannendoah, an Atlanta-based web designer with 15+ years of experience building sites for local businesses. The agency started as a web design practice — doing what it said, delivering sites that looked professional and launched on time.

The problem was visible early: clients had good websites that nobody could find. The sites didn't rank, didn't generate leads, and weren't solving the actual business problem. Adding SEO wasn't a business decision — it was necessary to make the work actually useful.

From there, the pattern kept repeating. Clients with strong web presence still had pipeline problems. They were ranking, getting traffic, but their leads came entirely from referrals they couldn't predict or scale. The lead generation service emerged the same way the SEO practice did: because clients needed it.

Today Social Linus focuses exclusively on B2B service companies — businesses where a single new client covers the investment many times over, and where the compounding effect of design, SEO, and lead generation working together is most pronounced.
